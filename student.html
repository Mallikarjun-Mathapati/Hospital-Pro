📖 How Does This Work?

This predictor uses a simple formula to estimate exam scores. More study hours and higher attendance generally lead to better scores!

Score = (Study Hours × 5) + (Attendance × 0.5)

Example: 6 hours study + 80% attendance = (6×5) + (80×0.5) = 30 + 40 = 70%
