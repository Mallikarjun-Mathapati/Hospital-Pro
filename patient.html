📊 How Risk Points Work

Each health factor gives 0, 1, or 2 risk points. The total determines your risk level.

Factor 0 Points (Low) 1 Point (Medium) 2 Points (High)
Age Under 30 30 - 50 Over 50
Blood Pressure Under 120 120 - 140 Over 140
Sugar Level Under 100 100 - 125 Over 125

Total Risk: 0-2 points = LOW | 3-4 points = MEDIUM | 5-6 points = HIGH
